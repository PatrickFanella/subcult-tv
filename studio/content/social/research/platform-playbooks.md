## Instagram

### How distribution works

- **There is no single Instagram algorithm.** Feed, Stories, Explore and Reels each rank differently. In Feed, your activity, information about the post, information about the poster and your history with them matter, roughly in that order; popularity counts for much more in Explore than in Feed. Published May 31, 2023. *[platform-documented]* ([Instagram, Ranking Explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained))
- **The three signals Mosseri names most: watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach.** In January 2025 he said likes matter slightly more for content shown to followers and sends (DM shares) slightly more for content shown to non-followers. Comments were not in that list. *[platform-documented, executive statement as reported]* ([Social Media Today, January 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/))
- **"A send is worth 3–5 likes" has no source.** Instagram has published no numeric weights; Mosseri's wording is "slightly more important". *[folklore/unverified]* ([Creator Lane review of the claim](https://creatorlanehq.com/blog/instagram-algorithm-2026-mosseri-vs-measured))
- **Original work is recommended; reposting is not.** April 2024: Instagram began replacing reposts with the original in recommendations and removing aggregator accounts from recommendations. *[platform-documented as reported]* ([TechCrunch, April 30, 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/04/30/instagram-is-updating-its-ranking-systems-to-surface-more-content-from-smaller-original-creators)) April 30, 2026: Mosseri said accounts that mostly post others' content are "no longer going to be recommendable"; commentary, remixing and credited collaboration count as transformation, watermarks and crops do not. The widely repeated "10 reposts in 30 days" threshold is not in that report. *[platform-documented as reported]* ([PetaPixel, April 30, 2026](https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/))
- **Raw beats polished as a trust signal, according to Instagram's head.** On December 31, 2025 Mosseri wrote that feeds were filling with synthetic media and that "raw" and "unflattering" images now signal a real person. *[platform-documented, executive opinion]* ([Engadget, December 31, 2025](https://www.engadget.com/social-media/instagram-chief-ai-is-so-ubiquitous-it-will-be-more-practical-to-fingerprint-real-media-than-fake-media-202620080.html)) For us: a real screen capture or a phone photo of the rig beats a glossy generated mockup.
- **Trial Reels test with non-followers first.** Launched December 10, 2024 for eligible professional accounts; shown only to non-followers, with results after about 24 hours. *[platform-documented]* ([Instagram Creators blog](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels))
- **Personalized guidance exists in the professional dashboard.** Meta's "Best Practices" hub (October 1, 2024) covers creation, engagement, reach and monetization per account. *[platform-documented]* ([Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/10/best-practices-education-hub-creators-instagram/)) Read it once the accounts are connected; generic advice is weaker than the account's own recommendations.

### Formats and limits

- **Hashtags: at most five per post or Reel, since December 2025.** Instagram's @creators account announced the cap and said a few specific tags work better than many generic ones; hashtags help categorize content for search rather than expand reach. *[platform-documented as reported]* ([Later](https://later.com/blog/ultimate-guide-to-using-instagram-hashtags/), [TechBuzz](https://www.techbuzz.ai/articles/instagram-caps-hashtags-at-five-to-combat-spam)) Our default stays zero to two specific tags.
- **Carousels hold up to 20 photos or videos** (since August 2024). *[platform-documented as reported]* ([MacRumors, August 8, 2024](https://www.macrumors.com/2024/08/08/instagram-20-photos-carousel-posts/))
- **Reels can run three minutes** (announced January 2025). *[platform-documented as reported]* ([MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2025/01/223-instagram-reels-3-minutes-us-tiktok-ban/)) A claim that Reels longer than three minutes are not recommended to non-followers circulates in creator guides; we did not find the primary statement. *[folklore/unverified]*
- **Profile grid shows 3:4 thumbnails** (since January 2025). Feed photos are accepted from 1.91:1 to 3:4 at up to 1080 px wide. *[platform-documented as reported]* ([Kapwing on the grid](https://www.kapwing.com/resources/instagrams-new-grid-layout-size-and-dimensions-2025/), [Instagram Help: image resolution](https://help.instagram.com/1631821640426723/)) The Instagram help page did not render for direct quoting during this check; Buffer's pages disagree on 3:4 support. See [visuals.md](visuals.md).
- **Captions are up to 2,200 characters, and roughly the first 125 show before "more" in the feed.** The 125 figure comes from Meta's ad specs for primary text; organic truncation varies by device and line breaks. *[practitioner consensus]* ([Strike Social summary of Meta ad specs](https://strikesocial.com/blog/meta-ad-specs/))
- **Caption links are not clickable.** Links work in Stories (link sticker) and the bio. *[platform behavior as reported]* ([Social Media Today, November 2024](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/heres-each-big-social-platform-stand-external-links/733946/)) Instagram posts should not depend on a URL; name the site plainly ("subcult.tv/kork") or point to the bio once.
- **Collab posts appear on each collaborator's profile and share one set of counts;** guides report up to five invited collaborators. *[practitioner consensus]* ([Buffer guide](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-collab-post/))
- **Alt text:** Advanced settings → Accessibility → Write alt text, editable after posting. *[platform-documented]* ([Instagram Help](https://help.instagram.com/503708446705527))

### What the vendor data says

- Buffer, March 2026 (52M+ posts across platforms): Instagram carousels earned about 12% more engagement than Reels; Reels reached about 36% more people. *[practitioner consensus, observational]* ([Buffer State of Engagement 2026](https://buffer.com/resources/state-of-social-media-engagement-2026/))
- Buffer, August 2025 (2.1M posts, 102K accounts, within-account models): moving from 1–2 to 3–5 feed posts a week was associated with more than double the follower growth rate and about 12% more reach per post; Buffer's own caveat is that content quality probably matters most. *[practitioner consensus, observational]* ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/))
- Buffer, January 2026 (700K posts, within-account): posts where the creator replied to comments had about 21% higher engagement; Buffer notes that well-performing posts attract more replies, so the direction is uncertain. *[practitioner consensus, observational]* ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-comments-engagement/))

### Playbook

1. One image or a short carousel built from real captures; the image does the stopping. Carousel when there is a sequence (before/after, three screens of a workflow).
2. First caption line carries the point and survives truncation: the product name or the specific thing, not a greeting.
3. Write for forwarding: "who would I send this to?" Sends drive reach to non-followers.
4. Zero to two specific hashtags. No link in the caption body unless named in plain text.
5. Original material only. Credit collaborators with a Collab post rather than reposting.
6. Reply to comments in a human window; do not automate replies.

## X

### What the open-sourced ranking code shows

xAI publishes the For You ranking code at [github.com/xai-org/x-algorithm](https://github.com/xai-org/x-algorithm) (repository created January 19, 2026; latest commit checked October 3, 2026). The README says cron jobs set the code defaults to the main production values. This replaces the 2023 `twitter/the-algorithm` release, whose heavy-ranker weights (reply 13.5, reply engaged by author 75, report −369) are now historical. *[platform-documented]* ([2023 weights, the-algorithm-ml](https://github.com/twitter/the-algorithm-ml/blob/main/projects/home/recap/README.md))

- **A transformer (Phoenix) predicts each viewer's chance of each action and sums weighted probabilities.** Predicted actions include favorite, reply, repost, quote, share, share via DM, share via copy link, clicks, dwell, follow author, and negatives: not interested, mute, block, report, not dwelled. The system "removes hand-engineered features". *[platform-documented]* ([README](https://github.com/xai-org/x-algorithm))
- **Current default weights** in [`home-mixer/params/param.rs`](https://github.com/xai-org/x-algorithm/blob/main/home-mixer/params/param.rs), read 2026-10-05: favorite 0.5, reply 5.0, repost 1.0, quote 5.0, share 2.0, share via DM 5.0, share via copy link 20.0, follow author 4.0, dwell 0.05, click 0.3, open link 0.2, profile click 0.0; not interested −47.52, block −31.2, mute −58.8, report −234.0. A bidirectional-follow reply boost of 15.0 adds weight to predicted replies on posts from mutual follows. *[platform-documented]*
- **Weights multiply probabilities, not counts.** The code comments say that reading "one report cancels 468 likes" from the ratios is wrong, because a report's baseline probability is over 1,000 times lower than a like's, and the predictions are personalized. *[platform-documented]* ([param.rs comments](https://github.com/xai-org/x-algorithm/blob/main/home-mixer/params/param.rs))
- **Post-scoring adjustments:** each additional post from the same author in a feed gets a decaying multiplier (author diversity); out-of-network posts are discounted; authors with few impressions get a boost toward a target position; posts older than 48 hours are filtered out. *[platform-documented]* ([README, Scoring and Filtering](https://github.com/xai-org/x-algorithm))
- **Mutual follows got a reply boost in July 2026.** X A/B tested values of 5–20 from July 10, rolled out 20 on July 13 and set 15 on July 24, 2026. *[platform-documented]* ([BIDIRECTIONAL_BOOST_CHANGE.md](https://github.com/xai-org/x-algorithm/blob/main/docs/BIDIRECTIONAL_BOOST_CHANGE.md))
- **Spam rules check URLs.** Rule files label posts containing low-quality URLs mentioned at non-followers as spam. *[platform-documented]* ([example rule](https://github.com/xai-org/x-algorithm/blob/main/botmaker-rules/scarecrow/bot/LQ_Tweets_With_LQ_URL_Verdict_At_Mention_To_NonFollower_v2.bot))

What we infer (not stated by X): a post that people would copy a link to, DM to a friend or reply to from a mutual follow scores well; a post that makes people tap "not interested" or mute is heavily penalized; posting several times in a burst competes with itself. These inferences are ours. *[practitioner inference from platform code]*

### Links, length, hashtags

- **X says the link penalty is gone.** On July 29, 2026, product head Nikita Bier told Mark Zuckerberg he no longer needed to put links in replies, and Elon Musk replied "We haven't for over a year." *[platform-documented, executive statement as reported]* ([Free Press Journal, July 29, 2026](https://www.freepressjournal.in/tech/x-product-head-nikita-bier-confirms-link-penalty-removed-over-a-year-ago-tells-mark-zuckerberg-he-can-post-them-directly)) Earlier, Bier attributed lower link reach to the in-app browser covering the post ([X, October 2025](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1979994223224209709)), and Musk said in 2024 that links get less boost because time spent on them is short ([Social Media Today, November 2024](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/heres-each-big-social-platform-stand-external-links/733946/)). Dwell is still a predicted action, so a post that is worth reading on its own still helps. The reported removal of an explicit penalty does not establish that link placement has no effect. A first self-reply is a testable tactic, not a proven universal improvement in traffic or conversion. *[practitioner inference; effect uncertain]*
- **Length:** 280 characters for accounts without Premium; Premium allows up to 25,000, and the timeline truncates around 280 with "Show more". *[platform-documented as reported]* ([X Premium help](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/x-premium), [Fedica summary](https://fedica.com/blog/schedule-long-posts-on-x-twitter-free/))
- **Hashtags:** X's own guidance recommends no more than two; a 2026 practitioner analysis of viral indie-game posts says hashtags no longer matter. *[platform-documented; practitioner consensus]* ([X Help, How to use hashtags](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/how-to-use-hashtags), [How To Market A Game, May 2026](https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/05/07/2026-tips-for-making-your-tweets-go-viral/))
- **Automation rules:** no duplicate or substantially similar posts across accounts one operator runs; automated posting about trending topics is prohibited; automated unsolicited replies are spam. *[platform-documented]* ([X automation rules](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/x-automation), [platform manipulation policy](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/authenticity))
- **Alt text:** up to 1,000 characters per image. *[platform-documented]* ([X Help](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/picture-descriptions))

### What the practitioner data says

- Buffer (1.7M posts, 2025): X, Threads and Bluesky all had a median of four engagements per post, but X's spread was far wider, so a typical post does little and a rare one does a lot. *[practitioner consensus, observational]* ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/x-threads-bluesky-data/))
- Buffer (2026 report, 2024–2025 data): median X engagement rates were text 3.56%, images 3.40%, video 2.96% and links 2.25%; replying to comments was associated with about 8% more engagement. These format comparisons are observational, not matched tests of adding an image or moving a link, and engagement is not traffic or conversion. *[practitioner consensus, observational]* ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/state-of-social-media-engagement-2026/))
- Indie-game marketer Chris Zukowski reports X link click-through of roughly 0.25–3% in his clients' data, and argues X works best for reaching people who amplify (journalists, streamers, other developers) rather than end users. *[practitioner consensus]* ([How To Market A Game, 2021](https://howtomarketagame.com/2021/02/08/how-to-use-twitter-to-market-your-game/)) His 2026 analysis of viral posts: evoke a feeling fast, open on the strongest visual, short clips not trailers. Sample: 20 posts, 8 games with conversion data. *[practitioner consensus, small sample]* ([2026 tips](https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/05/07/2026-tips-for-making-your-tweets-go-viral/))

### Playbook

1. One idea per post, front-loaded. Use an image when it shows the work or makes the offer clearer; text-only remains valid. Launch/supporter artwork and real product captures are local editorial preferences, not guaranteed reach improvements.
2. Write to earn a reply from people who know the subject, or a link-copy/DM share. A real question is allowed when we want the answers; a bait question is not.
3. Space posts out; a burst triggers the author-diversity decay.
4. The post must be worth reading without the click. Test inline versus first self-reply links separately on each account across several comparable posts; hold image format steady when testing placement. Compare actual visits and supporter actions where measurable alongside impressions and engagement. Keep the link inline unless the reply copy and provider/thread path are explicitly prepared and approved; never assume the reply travels with every impression.
5. Separate copy per account; never the same text from SUBCULT and Patrick.
6. Patrick replies by hand to people in the field; mutual-follow replies now carry extra weight.

## Bluesky

### Mechanics

- **Posts:** 300 graphemes of text, up to four images (each up to 2 MB, each with an `alt` field) or one video. Records can also carry up to eight hidden tags. *[platform-documented]* ([post lexicon](https://github.com/bluesky-social/atproto/blob/main/lexicons/app/bsky/feed/post.json), [images lexicon](https://github.com/bluesky-social/atproto/blob/main/lexicons/app/bsky/embed/images.json))
- **Video:** up to three minutes since March 10, 2025. *[platform-documented as reported]* ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/10/bluesky-now-lets-users-upload-videos-that-are-up-to-3-minutes-long/)) A report of a ten-minute limit from August 2026 surfaced in search; we did not verify it. *[folklore/unverified]*
- **Link cards are supplied by the poster.** The external-embed record stores the URI, title, description and an optional thumbnail up to 1 MB. A posting tool must fetch and attach card metadata itself, or the post shows a bare link. *[platform-documented]* ([external embed lexicon](https://github.com/bluesky-social/atproto/blob/main/lexicons/app/bsky/embed/external.json))
- **Bluesky says it does not demote links.** November 29, 2024: "we don't demote links." *[platform-documented]* ([Bluesky blog](https://bsky.social/about/blog/11-29-2024-engagement))
- **Feeds are a choice.** Custom feeds let anyone publish an algorithm; users pick which they follow (July 2023). *[platform-documented]* ([Bluesky blog](https://bsky.social/about/blog/7-27-2023-custom-feeds), [custom feed docs](https://docs.bsky.app/docs/starter-templates/custom-feeds)) Discover added "Show more/less like this" (May 2024). *[platform-documented as reported]* ([TechCrunch, May 10, 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/05/10/bluesky-now-lets-you-personalize-its-main-discover-feed-using-new-controls)) October 31, 2025: replies from a viewer's "social neighborhood" are prioritized, a private dislike is in testing, and toxic, spammy or off-topic replies are down-ranked. *[platform-documented]* ([Bluesky blog](https://bsky.social/about/blog/10-31-2025-building-healthier-social-media-update)) January 26, 2026: a dedicated Discover team and topic tags are planned. *[platform-documented]* ([Bluesky blog](https://bsky.social/about/blog/01-26-2026-whats-next-at-bluesky))
- **Starter packs:** up to 150 accounts and three custom feeds in a shareable link and QR code. *[platform-documented]* ([Bluesky blog, June 26, 2024](https://bsky.social/about/blog/06-26-2024-starter-packs)) An ICWSM 2025 study of 335,416 packs found they accounted for nearly 20% of all follows (up to 43% on peak migration days) and members gained up to 85% more followers than comparable users, mostly strengthening existing communities. *[research-backed]* ([Bootstrapping Social Networks, arXiv 2501.11605](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.11605))
- **Spam and bots:** the community guidelines forbid spam and repetitive disruptive posting; developer guidelines say a bot should interact with a user only when tagged. Scheduled posting is allowed. *[platform-documented]* ([Community Guidelines](https://bsky.social/about/support/community-guidelines), [Developer Guidelines](https://docs.bsky.app/docs/support/developer-guidelines))

### Culture

- **Alt text is a loud norm and a minority practice.** A July 2025 firehose sample of 2.85 million image posts found 19.9% had descriptions, up from about 0.1% on Twitter in 2019. *[research-backed, independent analysis]* ([Digital Seams](https://digitalseams.com/blog/image-descriptions-on-bluesky)) The app offers a "require alt text" setting; users publicly call out missing descriptions. *[practitioner consensus]* ([Fedica](https://fedica.com/blog/require-alt-text-bluesky/)) Missing alt text here costs more goodwill than on X.
- **Users are hostile to corporate presence.** Adobe's arrival in late 2024 drew a public pile-on; marketers compare the mood to early Reddit. *[practitioner consensus, journalism]* ([Creative Bloq](https://www.creativebloq.com/design/adobes-bluesky-rejection-proves-the-brand-is-woefully-out-of-touch-with-its-users), [Inc.](https://www.inc.com/sam-blum/is-a-brand-invasion-coming-for-bluesky-not-exactly/91019129)) A small collective that talks like people, credits others and does not sell hard fits; brand-speak does not.
- **Engagement is steadier and smaller.** Buffer: Bluesky posts had the narrowest engagement spread of X, Threads and Bluesky; replying was associated with about 5% more engagement. *[practitioner consensus, observational]* ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/x-threads-bluesky-data/), [Buffer 2026](https://buffer.com/resources/state-of-social-media-engagement-2026/))
- **Topic feeds often select by keyword or hashtag.** Getting into the right community feed (developer, music, left politics, Twitch) can matter more than the default Discover feed. *[practitioner consensus]* ([Agent Sky on custom feeds](https://useagentsky.com/blog/bluesky-custom-feeds-explained)) Check each feed's inclusion rule before relying on a tag.

### Playbook

1. Alt text on every image, written plainly.
2. One post, one point, under 300 graphemes; a short thread is fine when there is a sequence.
3. Links are welcome; attach a real card (title, description, thumbnail).
4. Use a specific tag only when a known feed uses it.
5. Build and join starter packs with peers (indie software, self-hosting, music, Twitch tools). Put other people in ours.
6. No bot replies, no follow-farming.

## Patreon

- **Free memberships and public posts are Patreon's growth path.** Free membership launched June 2023. Patreon's help center says public posts help creators appear in fans' discovery feeds and that creators saw a "nearly 3x" increase in community growth without a decrease in new paid member growth; it does not publish the method. *[platform-documented, method unstated]* ([Patreon Help: free memberships](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/16433886029325-How-free-memberships-can-help-grow-your-community), [TechCrunch, June 2023](https://techcrunch.com/2023/06/21/patreon-adds-e-commerce-tools-free-fan-membership/))
- **Patreon added community chats and a creator-organized home page** in October 2023. *[platform-documented]* ([Patreon, October 4, 2023](https://news.patreon.com/articles/patreon-reimagined))
- **Patreon recommends a steady cadence of public posts and gives no number.** *[platform-documented]* ([Patreon creator hub, free content strategy](https://creatorhub.patreon.com/fr-FR/articles/how-to-build-a-free-content-strategy)) "Four posts a week" and similar figures in third-party guides have no traceable source. *[folklore/unverified]* ([example of the claim](https://influencermarketinghub.com/patreon-memberships/))
- **Behind-the-scenes material is a common benefit:** rough drafts, tools, process. *[platform-documented]* ([Patreon creator hub](https://creatorhub.patreon.com/articles/9-strategies-to-boost-member-retention-rates-with-meaningful-benefits))
- **Patrons respond to relationship, not just files.** Qualitative research finds Patreon creators frame support as a social bond and reward it with disclosure and contact. *[research-backed, qualitative]* ([Hair 2021](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/348821620_Friends_not_ATMs_parasocial_relational_work_and_the_construction_of_intimacy_by_artists_on_Patreon), [Bonifacio, Hair & Wohn](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/14614448211027961))

**Playbook.** VOICE.md sets the tone: a frank ledger, "we post when we have something to share, not on a schedule". That does not conflict with Patreon's advice if the edition says what kind of rhythm to expect without promising dates. Each edition: what we worked on, what it cost or what broke, what support paid for, one specific thing a supporter can try or answer. Public editions double as discovery; patron-only material is the working detail (drafts, failed approaches, numbers). No gratitude theatre, no tier-as-experience copy.

## Website devlog

- **People scan web pages; front-load and chunk.** 79% of Nielsen's 1997 test users scanned; concise, scannable rewrites improved usability (1997). The F-pattern persisted in 2017 on desktop and mobile. Headings, short paragraphs and descriptive first words help. *[research-backed]* ([NN/g 1997](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/how-users-read-on-the-web/), [NN/g 2017](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/text-scanning-patterns-eyetracking/), [First 2 Words](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/first-2-words-a-signal-for-scanning/))
- **A regular devlog builds a returning audience.** Factorio's "Friday Facts" have run weekly since 2013 with technical detail and plans. *[practitioner consensus]* ([Friday Facts #1](https://factorio.com/blog/post/fff-1), [seven-year retrospective](https://spieswl.github.io/blog/2020/seven-years-of-factorio-friday-facts)) Indie-dev guidance: have something visual first, keep a cadence readers can learn, and write about problems and how they were solved. *[practitioner consensus]* ([indiegamedev.net](https://indiegamedev.net/2020/02/05/how-to-write-a-good-devlog/))
- **Show the real thing.** Players distinguish glamour shots from actual gameplay and want the latter. *[practitioner consensus]* ([Xsolla on Zukowski](https://accelerator.xsolla.com/blog/howtomarketagame))
- **Communities that link to devlogs have rules.** Show HN accepts things people can try, not blog posts or sign-up pages, and forbids asking friends to upvote. *[platform-documented]* ([Show HN guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html))

**Playbook.** The devlog is the canonical long version; social posts point to it. Title says what changed for whom. Open with the person and problem, then what is different, then what is still broken or unfinished, then how to try it. One real capture near the top with alt text. Date every post. Keep the maturity label. A social post that links here must still make sense to someone who never clicks.
