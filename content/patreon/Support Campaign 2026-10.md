# Support Campaign — October 2026

> [!note] Offer superseded October 2, 2026
> The owner requested no promised posting schedule, shared light rewards with private Discord access and occasional BTS/exclusive releases, and Supporter $5, Backer $10, Patron $25, Groceries $400 and Rent $1,500. Use the [support redesign](support-redesign-2026-10/README.md) for proposed current copy and site guidelines. The earlier offer and timeline below are retained as dated planning history; do not reuse their weekly/monthly promises or tier-retirement instructions. Patreon publication, website updates and Discord access verification remain pending. Existing tasks and timers were not changed.

> [!abstract] Start support from zero: relaunch Patreon as the workshop behind the six public products, give people a free way to follow first, and send a monthly newsletter that turns a month of real work into one reason to support it.

## Starting point (checked 2026-10-01)

- Patreon: 0 patrons. Seven published tiers, from Free to "Rent" at $1,425. Backer ($10) promises early access and Sponsor ($15) private channels. The [[Patreon Relaunch 2026-08|August relaunch]] checklist was never completed.
- Newsletter: Brevo free plan (300 emails a day). List 2, "SUBCULT.TV Newsletter", has 1 contact. Three earlier subcult.tv sign-ups sit only in the site database. Sign-ups now use double opt-in.
- subcult.tv Support page says supporters get no early access or private status. That contradicts both the live Patreon tiers and the chosen model below.
- Social: the scheduled runner drafts weekly posts from the [[SUBCULT Social Content Idea Pool]]. The first pilot week is 2026-W41 (October 5–11). Posting stays manual through Buffer.

## Decisions (owner, 2026-10-01)

- **Offer:** the workshop model from [[Patreon Positioning]]. A free follow, plus Backer at $10/month for weekly field notes, a monthly studio ledger and selected early previews. The products stay public, and support buys no ownership, governance or release guarantee.
- **Newsletter:** monthly.
- **Channels:** email (Brevo), Patreon, subcult.tv and social through the existing runner.
- **Approval:** the owner approved this plan on 2026-10-01; drafts and schedules follow from it.
- **Links:** Patreon links use the custom domain https://support.subcult.tv. Support is back in the subcult.tv navigation.
- **Mailing address:** the owner will get a PO box. No marketing email is scheduled until the Brevo account address is updated, because US law requires it in every footer.

## Goal and measures

Primary goal: first paying Backers. Secondary: a list of people who chose to hear from SUBCULT.

These are targets to test, not forecasts. By the 2026-11-08 review:

| Measure | Target | Source |

| Paid Backers | 3 | Patreon |
| Free Patreon follows | 15 | Patreon |
| Confirmed newsletter subscribers | 30 | Brevo list 2 |
| Support page visits | Baseline only | Umami, once enabled |

Judge the month on the [[Patreon Relaunch 2026-08#Thirty-day success gate|thirty-day gate]]: four useful field notes and a ledger delivered without strain matter more than any single number.

## Message

One line everywhere:

> The products stay public. Patreon is the workshop: weekly field notes, a monthly ledger, and early previews. Follow free, or back it at $10/month.

Every ask names a real artifact from that week, such as a product capture, a decision or a fix. Claims follow the [[Product Claims Register]]: Patchwork is a pre-alpha demonstration, and undated or unverified features are not named.

## Monthly newsletter

- **When:** the first Tuesday of each month, 10:00 Chicago.
- **From:** SUBCULT.TV <noreply@subcult.tv>, replies to info@subcult.tv.
- **Shape:** under 300 words. What shipped, one featured product with a capture, what's in development, one support ask, and the unsubscribe and mailing-address footer Brevo adds.
- **Sources:** the month's studio ledger, public posts and the claims register. Nothing appears that isn't public or verified.
- **List:** Brevo list 2. Sign-ups come from subcult.tv through double opt-in.
