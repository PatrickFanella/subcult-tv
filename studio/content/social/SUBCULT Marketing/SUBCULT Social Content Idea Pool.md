This is the candidate bank for the seven active product lanes and occasional Common House teasers. Use the [project marketing strategies](../../marketing/README.md) to choose an audience, theme, action and demonstration; a candidate is a possible story within that strategy. Every entry below is `candidate`: no final copy, media or scheduling approval is implied. Each premise is written in its product's voice ([voice guide](../../VOICE.md)); the posting account still decides who is speaking. ## Turning a candidate into a post

The current week's note owns exact account-specific copy and final assets. Preserve the candidate ID and include: state; claim ID; sources; evidence date; account/channel; final copy; final media/alt text; local date/time; expiry; reuse policy; approval; delivery record. Approval binds the exact version. Drafting and a checked claim do not authorize publication.

Patrick explains a decision or consequence. SUBCULT shows the product artifact and a useful next action. The CLPR account covers CLPR only. Instagram adapts a ready visual; Patreon explains the same work in more depth. Do not copy one caption to all accounts.

## Rotation and selection

Start the first full pilot week with the strongest ready product. Across the pilot, aim to give each of clpr, Patchwork, HasanAra, Rekolekt, Subcult OS, Undertow and Kork one anchor. Kork joined on October 2, 2026; with seven products and six pilot weeks, one week can carry two anchors or one product can wait for the next cycle. Readiness can reorder or skip a lane. No product has its own mandatory weekly quota.

The Common House can replace one Friday post at most once per calendar month when an asset is ready. It is not an additional weekly obligation. Music, plugin launches and older campaigns are deferred outside this active bank; their original drafts remain recoverable.
