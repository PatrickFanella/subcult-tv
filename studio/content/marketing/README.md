Prepared October 21, 2026. Working direction for preparation and owner review. Audience descriptions are hypotheses to test. This does not approve copy, outreach, schedules or publication.

Packet ownership, revision loading, evidence registration and review decisions are documented in [OPERATING.md](OPERATING.md). The selected packet registry is [packets.json](packets.json).

The maintained runtime input is [strategies.json](strategies.json). It covers the 24 projects in the website catalogue and The Common House. Product repositories own technical claims; Studio owns campaign direction, voice and media. The [claims register](../social/Product%20Claims%20Register.md) remains the fact boundary and [VOICE](../VOICE.md) remains the voice owner.

## How preparation changes

Start with a project, a reader task and a content theme. Look for a demonstrated result or a useful explanation, then choose a format. Recent merged changes can supply evidence; they are not the campaign calendar. If collection fails, record that failure and keep it out of factual copy. An evergreen showcase can continue using its independent claims and current public page.

Weekly X planning receives the portfolio strategies. The timely lane still handles genuinely timely material; an empty timely run is acceptable. Daily Instagram uses the same strategy pack and rotates themes within the selected project. Existing account capacities, the two-day showcase gap and owner approval remain in force. More projects do not multiply those limits. A working strategy is not permission to promote a preview as a public service.

See the [initial evaluation and activation record](evaluation-2026-10-21.md) for the writing comparison, regression evidence and remaining limits.

## First four weeks

1. Prepare one complete first-use demonstration for each of clpr, Undertow, Kork and HasanAra, paced within the existing account caps. Choose one task per asset. Recheck the actual destination, account conditions, claims and source rights. A missing recording becomes a private capture task, never an invented result.
2. Draft a demonstration and a different useful explanation from each ready asset. Offer the owner alternatives; do not post the same words on every channel. Keep a map from source claim and asset to each derivative. Instagram needs an image that matches its central task; short text can stand alone.
3. Use approval edits, rejections, distinct replies and available product actions to choose the next theme. Revisit a preview or developer tool only when its labeled demonstration and destination are ready. Community posts and personal outreach are proposals until separately authorized.
4. Review after two comparable approved pieces per tested theme. Record effort, available impressions/clicks, useful actions and feedback. Missing analytics remain unavailable. Retain a theme that brings useful actions or actionable feedback; fix a confusing journey before increasing distribution. Small samples guide the next experiment, not a conversion claim.

No spend is proposed. Campaign start is the first owner-approved piece. The plan supplies choices for existing slots rather than a second publishing schedule.

## clpr

**Audience hypothesis:** A viewer trying to find a clip they remember and send it to a friend.

**Positioning:** Find the clip, keep the collection, send one link.

**Themes:** Find a clip by creator and topic; Build a playlist around one shared interest; Share a collection with someone who missed the stream.

**First-use asset:** Search → open clip → save to playlist → open its public link in another session.

**Action:** Explore clips → https://clpr.tv/. **Evidence:** `C-CLPR` in the claims register or catalogue source.

**Limits:** No completeness, ingestion-speed, ranking or native-app claims; repeat authenticated playlist sharing.

**Measure:** Search sessions followed by a clip open / search sessions; completed saves / save attempts. Baseline unavailable. **Preparation:** existing showcase lane.
