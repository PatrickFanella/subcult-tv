Sus quotient: four names and directions

Candidates for naming and branding the Twitch bot-signal dashboard. Each pairs a name with a mark, palette, type and the colour ramp the charts use for the quotient. Marks are original; channel names in the artwork are bars; numbers and headlines are sample copy. Nothing here is approved for publication. Open the comparison sheet.

A / Headcount: the door clicker

The tally a door person keeps at a show. Four strokes and the slash; two strokes are hollow, people counted who never came in. Night ink, counter amber, Barlow Condensed numerals. Neutral name: it describes counting, not accusing. Common English word, so harder to own in search.

B / Papered: the house that was papered

Theatre slang: to paper the house is to hand out free tickets so a show looks sold out. A ticket stub with a seat plan, some seats only outlined. Playbill paper, house red, Fraunces. The most memorable name and the most pointed; it implies someone did the papering.

C / Sus Quotient: the gauge

Keeps the working name and makes the score the logo: a 0 to 100 gauge with the top band marked and the needle in it. Instrument black, flag coral, cyan for the chatter line, Space Grotesk. "Sus" is native to the audience and reads as a joke; the score has to carry the seriousness.

D / Ghostcount: the crowd with holes in it

A crowd as a dot grid; some dots are rings, viewers with nobody there. Dusk plum, ectoplasm lime, Inter Tight and IBM Plex Mono. Playful without saying "bot" or "sus", and unused as a product name in our search. Ghost viewer is already Twitch slang for a silent viewer.
