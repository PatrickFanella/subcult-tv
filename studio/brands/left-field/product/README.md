# Left Field — Web assets

The approved LF favicon, a 180-pixel Apple touch icon, 192- and 512-pixel app icons,
a 1200×630 share card and a 1600×1000 hero illustration. All are exported from the pack logo and banner
masters. The Left Field site carries the favicon, touch icon, both app icons, the
share card and the outlined hero. `WEBSITE.md` lists where each is used.
