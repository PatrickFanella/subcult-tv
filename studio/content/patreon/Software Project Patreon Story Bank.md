## Current offer and activation boundaries

The owner confirmed October 6 that all five paid tiers retain the same benefits, including early access, playtests/feedback/experimental drops, occasional polls and optional acknowledgment, alongside supporter posts, private Discord channels and occasional behind-the-scenes material or exclusive releases. This supersedes the earlier lighter benefit proposal. There is no fixed delivery schedule, guaranteed reply, commission, consulting service or roadmap control. Credits require explicit opt-in; a poll invites input rather than assigning decisions to members.

The October 6 creator inspection found all five tier banners loading and the rewritten About saved. The support rewrite is live at https://subcult.tv/support. Discord is connected, but no roles were assigned to the five tiers and the role selectors were disabled. A payout method and relaunch welcome post still need setup; a true logged-out/member journey has not passed. These observations are dated, not permanent holds: recheck the live activation checklist in Super Productivity before using benefit claims in a public campaign.

Until Discord role mapping and member access are tested, a draft may describe that setup as pending or point to a verified public artifact. It must not advertise working private-channel access. Early access, playtests and releases need an actual available item before a post says what a member can try. The owner still reviews, schedules and publishes every campaign draft.
