# SUBCULT messaging one-sheet

## Current proposition

Software for independent culture.

SUBCULT builds software for clip curation, searchable archives, mutual aid experiments, live events, music visuals and link pages. The catalog and publishing rotation focus on clpr, Patchwork, HasanAra, Rekolekt, Subcult OS, Undertow and Kork. The Common House appears now and then as a game in development.

Take each product's scope and maturity from [[001 Projects/Works/Social Posts/Product Claims Register]], and its voice from the [voice guide](../../VOICE.md). “Active development” does not mean production-ready. Describe the specific artifact. Do not promise that everything under the SUBCULT name is production-ready, community-owned, privacy-guaranteed, self-sustaining or uniformly licensed.

Suggested compact bio, awaiting approval before any account is updated: “Independent software for clips, archives, mutual aid, live events, music visuals and link pages. Built by SUBCULT.” The October 2 revision added link pages for Kork. The earlier wording is still valid if the owner prefers it.

## Product destinations

As of October 2: use https://subcult.tv/products and its six product landings. Send people to clpr.tv, hasanara.tv and undertow.subcult.tv for the public apps. Patchwork is a demonstration, not a service for real help. Rekolekt and Subcult OS currently use repository and contact paths. Kork (launched October 2, 2026) is live at https://kork.subcult.tv but had no subcult.tv catalog landing that day, so link to the app directly.

Note, October 3: the current subcult-tv source (`src/lib/showcase.ts`, checked at `0a09bb1`) lists nine products, adding Prichal, Kork and Left Field to the six above. The live deployment was not checked. Until it is, keep linking Kork directly. Prichal and Left Field are not in the publishing rotation and have no claims register entry.

## Funding and participation

Support may help with hosting, tools and independent production. Do not describe a proposed funding route as money already received. Public code does not imply community ownership or governance across SUBCULT. Specific licenses, contribution paths, prices and supporter benefits need current evidence.

[[SUBCULT Funding and Partnership Policy]] is the policy reference. The state of the Patreon creator page was not verified, so automated copy must avoid prices, promises of published posts and benefit guarantees.
