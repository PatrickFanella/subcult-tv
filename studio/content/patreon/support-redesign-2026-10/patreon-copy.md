# Patreon page and shared rewards

Review draft, October 2, 2026. Prices are proposed USD monthly membership amounts; Patreon checkout establishes the final charge. No new tiers have been published.

## Membership structure

Use Patreon's free follow option plus five paid tiers. Lead with Supporter, Backer and Patron; present Groceries and Rent after them as optional larger contributions. Give every paid tier the same supporter access. Highlight Backer as the middle everyday contribution option, without calling it the most popular tier or implying extra privileges.

| Option | Monthly contribution | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Follow free | $0 | Public posts and announcements shared on Patreon |
| Supporter | $5 | Private supporter Discord channels; supporter posts; occasional BTS material and exclusive releases when shared |
| Backer | $10 | Same access as Supporter; a larger contribution |
| Patron | $25 | Same access as Supporter; a larger contribution |
| Groceries | $400 | Same access as Supporter; optional larger contribution with a food-budget joke |
| Rent | $1,500 | Same access as Supporter; optional larger contribution with a rent-budget joke |

One pool of paid rewards keeps delivery manageable. Discord is the concrete ongoing benefit; posts and releases are occasional. There is no weekly, monthly or minimum release commitment. Access means access to the designated channels and published member material; it does not promise personal availability or replies.

## About copy

SUBCULT makes independent software, music and creative work. Our projects include tools for finding clips, searching recording archives, making music visuals and organizing live events.

Support helps cover hosting, tools and the time spent building and maintaining the work.

Paid members can join private channels in our Discord and read supporter posts on Patreon. We may also share behind-the-scenes material, works in progress and occasional exclusive releases. These arrive when we have something to share, with no fixed publishing schedule.

Supporter ($5), Backer ($10) and Patron ($25) have the same core access. Groceries and Rent are there too, if you're feeling extraordinarily generous. Same rewards, larger contribution. Choose the amount that works for you.

Public products and their public releases stay available on their own terms. Patreon gives you a closer view of the studio and occasional member material; it does not unlock paid product plans, commissions or private consulting.

Follow for free for public posts, or join a paid tier to support the work.

## Benefit configuration

| Patreon benefit | Set on tiers | Delivery rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Private Discord channels | All five paid tiers | Use one shared supporter role; check access for every tier before advertising it |
| Supporter posts | All five paid tiers | Publish to all paid members; no cadence promise |
| Occasional BTS material | All five paid tiers | Share only selected material when ready |
| Occasional exclusive releases | All five paid tiers | State the file, access, permitted use and whether exclusivity is temporary in each release |

Keep supporter credits and advisory questions optional editorial choices. If credits are offered, collect explicit opt-in and the exact display name; never export member names by default. Questions invite input without assigning roadmap control. Neither should appear as a recurring benefit.

Exclusive release candidates can include an owned music edit, a selected Ableton rack, a process download or a work-in-progress preview. Verify rights and remove private material before sharing. Each downloadable release should state whether personal use, modification, commercial use or redistribution is allowed; membership alone does not grant those permissions. Do not promise access to all past files until the archive has been checked.
