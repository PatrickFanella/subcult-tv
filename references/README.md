# Design References

Design-system studies and their supporting evidence. This repository owns the reports; Subcult Studio retains its production brand packs.

- [Website collection — 2026-10-02](2026-10-02-websites/README.md): 37 site studies, measured token observations, source notes, screenshots and artifact manifests. [Open the gallery](2026-10-02-websites/index.html).
- [Seven-style research and Refero review — 2026-10-02](2026-10-02-style-research/README.md): Y2K, Neo-Brutalism, Memphis, Swiss, hand drawn, retro and minimalist references, with 24 design-system studies and measured token observations. [Open the style gallery](2026-10-02-style-research/index.html).
- [Experimental Sound Studio](ESS/design-system.md): the earlier ESS design-system report and supporting evidence.

The website collection follows Studio’s separation of written guides, tokens, previews and provenance. See [how the references map to Studio](2026-10-02-websites/STUDIO-FORMAT.md) and [verification limits](2026-10-02-websites/VERIFICATION.md) before adapting a study.

These reports reconstruct public interfaces from dated observations. They are not official design systems or production-ready brand packs. Third-party assets retain their original ownership; individual guides record rendering and inspection limits.

[The Refero downloader](scripts/README.md) archives public DESIGN.md exports and their displayed screenshots into `refero-archive/`. It is resumable and keeps downloaded source material separate from the original studies.
