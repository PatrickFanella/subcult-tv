# Monthly Promotion Review

Use this to improve the Patreon pitch and promotion loop.

## Monthly tasks

1. Publish Patreon roadmap.
2. Run patron poll.
3. Share public “month in projects” recap.
4. Thank patrons.
5. Update Patreon pitch based on performance.

## Review questions

- Which [[001 Projects/Works/Patreon/changemaker]] posts drove replies, saves, or clicks?
- Which [[001 Projects/Works/Patreon/subcult.tv]] topics produced the best social content?
- Which CTA performed best: soft, medium, or direct?
- Which platform generated most Patreon traffic?
- What did patrons respond to most: process, tools, drafts, commentary, tutorials?
- What should be repeated next month?
- What should be cut?

## Metrics to track

- Patreon visits
- patron conversions
- post comments
- poll participation
- X engagement and link clicks
- Instagram Story taps/link clicks
- Reel watch time
- TikTok watch time
- blog-to-Patreon traffic

## Pitch update checklist

- tighten [[001 Projects/Works/Patreon/Patreon Positioning]]
- update pinned post if needed
- rotate CTA language
- double down on top-performing post themes
- schedule next month in [[001 Projects/Works/Patreon/Super Productivity Promotion Tasks]]
