## What the reference shows

Illustrated faces and small doodled objects surround otherwise conventional typography and product screenshots. Selective colored backgrounds make the line drawings feel like annotations.

## What to document next

Define illustration stroke, fills, scale and placement separately from ordinary controls; do not make every interface element appear hand-written.

## Evidence and differences

The reference supports the illustration layer. It does not establish a hand-drawn application UI or the motion timing claimed in the source.

The saved image is supplied by Refero. It was retrieved and visually inspected for this research; its original capture date is unknown. It is not a new screenshot of the live site. Refero’s written system is interpreted and normalized, so its roles and recommendations remain secondary evidence.

[Live computed-style observation](live-observation.json) records the actual page URL, viewport and sampled values.

The study does not verify mobile behavior, accessibility, complete motion, inner-page components or task completion. A complete system guide should inspect those states before establishing global tokens. No font binaries, logos or production identity assets were copied.
