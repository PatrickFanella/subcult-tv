# LOW, rebuilt for a longer night

## Post copy

I've been working on a dark electronic version of Cracker's "Low." This is the extended club mix.

I isolated the original vocal with Ableton's stem separation and built a new arrangement around it. The song's repeating four-chord structure gave me something steady to work against. The changes could happen in the drums, the bass, and how much of the arrangement was present at any moment.

The house version was the one I wanted to keep developing. I wanted more pump from the bass, more percussion up top, and little sounds that answer the vocal or appear once and disappear. The extended arrangement gives those parts time to come in, build, and drop away before the full groove returns.

There are longer builds and breakdowns here, but I didn't want the extra length to mean repeating the same section without changing anything. Some of the work was adding things. Some was deciding when the kick and bass were enough.

This version uses Cracker's original vocal. It's my remix and additional production, not a newly recorded performance of the vocal.

Where does the longer arrangement hold your attention, and where would you cut it back? A timestamp would be useful.
