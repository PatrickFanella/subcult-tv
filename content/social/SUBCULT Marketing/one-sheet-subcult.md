# SUBCULT Messaging One-Sheet

## Current proposition

Software for independent culture.

SUBCULT builds software for clip curation, searchable archives, mutual aid experiments, live events and music visuals. Focus the catalog and publishing rotation on clpr, Patchwork, HasanAra, Rekolekt, Subcult OS and Undertow. The Common House appears occasionally as a game in development.

Use [[001 Projects/Works/Social Posts/Product Claims Register]] for each product's scope and maturity. “Active development” does not establish production readiness. Describe the specific artifact instead of promising that the entire umbrella is production-ready, community-owned, privacy-guaranteed, self-sustaining or uniformly licensed.

Suggested compact bio, awaiting account update approval: “Independent software for clips, archives, mutual aid, live events and music visuals. Built by SUBCULT.”

## Product destinations

Use https://subcult.tv/products and its six product landings. Direct users to clpr.tv, hasanara.tv and undertow.subcult.tv for their public apps. Patchwork is a demonstration, not a real-help service. Rekolekt and Subcult OS currently use repository/contact paths.

## Funding and participation

Support may help with hosting, tools and independent production. Do not describe proposed funding routes as money already received. Public code does not imply community ownership or umbrella-wide governance. Specific licenses, contribution paths, prices and supporter benefits require current evidence.

Keep [[SUBCULT Funding and Partnership Policy]] as a policy reference. Patreon creator-page state was not verified; avoid prices, published-post promises or benefit guarantees in automated copy.
