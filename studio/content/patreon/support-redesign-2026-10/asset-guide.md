## Platform and crop checks

Patreon's [creator-page settings](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026139111-Customize-your-creator-page), read October 2, specify 2500 × 625 for the new page, 1600 × 400 for the legacy page and a square profile image of at least 1024 × 1024. Its [image-size guide](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/360038982432-How-do-I-size-images-for-Patreon-posts) lists tier images at 460 × 200 and feed images at 1920 × 1080. Editor cropping still needs a real preview; local rendering does not prove Patreon placement.

Upload PNGs, check phone and desktop views, then adjust the source if the active editor crops the headline or mark. The header leaves open space around the mark and message but does not claim a universally safe crop. Do not assume Patreon lets you change body fonts; the brand fonts are baked into artwork, and platform text uses Patreon's own controls.

Dark ink on purple measures about 4.58:1; dark ink on green has ample body-text contrast. Green registration bars are decorative. The tier images name the tier only; their accessible descriptions and adjacent text must explain the actual offer.
