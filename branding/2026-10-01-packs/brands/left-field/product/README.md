# Left Field — Web assets

Approved LF favicon, 180-pixel Apple touch icon, 192/512-pixel app icons and
a 1200×630 share card and a 1600×1000 hero illustration. These exports derive from the pack logo and banner
masters. The Left Field site carries the favicon, touch icon, both app icons, the
share card and the outlined hero; `WEBSITE.md` lists where each is used.
