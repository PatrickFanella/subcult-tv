# New SUBCULT bumps: the fair at dusk

I went to the fair at dusk with a phone and came back with enough footage for a set of SUBCULT bumps.

The rides did most of the work for me: saturated lights, metal structures against the evening sky, the ferris wheel, a cross-shaped silhouette. A few shots land somewhere between a local fair and a broadcast from somewhere else.

I've cut the footage into six short bump videos for `subcult.tv`:

- `subcult-ferris.mp4`
- `subcult-ufo.mp4`
- `subcult-cross.mp4`
- `subcult-cross-shadow.mp4`
- `subcult-collage.mp4`
- `subcult-top-secret.mp4`

The picture is mostly there. Sound is the next pass.

I don't want to leave these as silent loops, so the open question is how much the music should explain the images. One direction is minimal and atmospheric: a short signal, a drone, or a pulse, and the fair footage stays strange. The other is more rhythmic and synthetic, which pushes the clips toward title sequences or broadcast bumps.

Right now I'm leaning toward short, tactile tracks that don't quite resolve. The footage already has plenty of color and motion. The music needs to make six clips feel like one set without turning them into the same piece.

I want to keep posting small experiments like this here: a real place, a few minutes of footage, and what I decided to do with it.

Next I'll score the first two clips, compare the tone, and carry whichever musical rule works better across the rest of the set.

**Question for patrons:** eerie late-night transmission, or bright synthetic fairground?
