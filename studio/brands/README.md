# Brands

Each folder is one brand's production pack: `GUIDE.md`, tokens in JSON and CSS, logos, social and video layouts, fonts with licenses, alt-text starters and a hash-bound `asset-manifest.json`. Some packs add product assets (`product/`), a design system (`design-system/`), a landing page or a `WEBSITE.md` describing how the live site uses them. Open [the gallery](index.html) for every pack side by side.

The ten packs marked proposed are smaller site packs for the project pages: a mark, lockup, hero, link preview, sample social layouts, tokens and a guide, generated on October 12, 2026 from `brand-tools/site-packs.json` and not yet reviewed by the owner.

Packs are generated. Change a brand through its generator and masters in [brand tools](../brand-tools/README.md), then rebuild that brand alone; do not edit PNG exports by hand. Backline and Subsets build from the `build_assets.py` or `build.py` inside their own folders. Direction studies and reviews that led to these choices are in [studies](../studies/).
