# Patreon Posting Strategy

Use Patreon as the deeper process layer behind public output.

## Cadence

### Weekly

Publish one substantial field note sourced from real work:

- a build decision and its consequence
- a failure, diagnosis, and changed rule
- an annotated preview or implementation note
- a research trail or cut section
- a roadmap choice with its scope stated clearly

End with one specific question when a response would genuinely affect the next step.

### Monthly

Publish one studio ledger:

- what shipped
- what changed
- what cost more than expected
- what failed or stopped
- what support enabled
- what comes next

Add a roadmap or advisory question only when the underlying Work has a verified current state.

## Recurring post themes

- Behind the [[001 Projects/Works/Patreon/changemaker]]
- Project field notes
- Drafts, fragments, prototypes
- [[001 Projects/Works/Patreon/subcult.tv]] blog companion posts
- Roadmap notes
- Postmortems
- Patron polls

## Community loop

Ask one specific question per patron post.

Close the loop later:

> Last week patrons voted for X. Here’s what I’m doing with that.

## Cadence gate

Keep the weekly field note and monthly ledger for four weeks before increasing frequency. Posting volume is not a success measure if it weakens the work, backlog, or health behind the posts.
