## Design direction

These design notes and exercises are SUBCULT interpretations, rather than specifications supplied by the museum.

Treat the homepage as a designed cover: one identity, a clear directory and a few graphic landmarks.

| Element | Suggested treatment |

| Layout | Compact masthead; grouped destinations |

## Where to use it

A label homepage, fan archive or event microsite.
