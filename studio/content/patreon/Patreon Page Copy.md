# Patreon Page Copy

> [!abstract] Public Patreon copy for the Support route. Patreon funds the workshop and provides named benefits; it does not sell ownership, governance, guaranteed releases, or control of the roadmap.

## Page headline

# Enter the workshop

Most of what SUBCULT makes stays public. Patreon is the deeper operational layer: build notes, early previews, drafts, experiments, and the decisions behind the work.

## Short bio

SUBCULT builds open, inspectable software, media systems, research tools, and participatory experiments.

Follow the public archive for finished work. Support on Patreon for the process behind it—and to create more room for independent development, documentation, and publishing.

## Full About copy

SUBCULT is an underground creative-technical collective building systems for culture, coordination, and strange futures.

The public side holds the projects, releases, zine, repositories, and durable archive. Patreon opens a closer view of the workshop: build logs, planning notes, early previews, drafts, technical decisions, failed experiments, and occasional advisory questions about bounded project choices.

Support helps pay for hosting, tools, research, design, accessibility, documentation, and production time. It does not buy ownership, governance, a guaranteed release schedule, or control over unrelated projects.

Follow for free if you want the public signal. Choose a paid tier if the process itself is useful to you and you want to help sustain it.

## CTA block

> Support the work. Read the machinery.

- Follow for public updates and occasional previews.
- Join a paid tier for the specific benefits listed on that tier.
- Leave whenever the signal stops being useful.

## Relaunch membership architecture

Start with free following and one paid tier. Add another paid tier only after at least four weeks of delivery and member response show a distinct, sustainable benefit.

### Free

For people who want to follow the public signal.

- Public updates and occasional previews.
- Invitations to read, inspect, test, or respond when a Work has a concrete participation path.

### Backer — $10/month

For people who want the process behind the public work and want to help sustain it.

- One substantial field note each week.
- One monthly studio ledger: what shipped, changed, cost, failed, and comes next.
- Selected early previews, drafts, implementation notes, and research trails when they exist.
- Occasional advisory questions whose scope and effect are stated in advance.
- Access to the supporter archive.

Backer does not promise daily output, unlimited access, private consulting, governance rights, or that every roadmap item will ship.

## Future-tier gate

Possible future tiers such as Workshop or Underwriter remain design options, not launch commitments. Define the added benefit, delivery cost, rights boundary, and exit condition before publishing one.

## Benefit rules

- Replace “occasional chances to shape what ships next” with **advisory input on explicitly bounded choices**.
- A poll is nonbinding unless it names the eligible voters, decision rule, scope, and binding effect.
- “Early access” means access before a public release when a releasable artifact exists; it is not a release guarantee.
- “Closer line” means a defined group channel, office hour, or feedback thread—not unlimited direct access.
- Never promise daily or monthly output unless the operating cadence can sustain it.
- High-dollar tiers fund the operation; they do not silently become consulting retainers or governance seats.

## Funding transparency block

SUBCULT may also use earned revenue, grants, sponsorships, partnerships, or aligned investment. Patreon support remains patronage: it helps fund the work and provides the listed benefits, but it is not an investment and carries no expectation of repayment or ownership.
