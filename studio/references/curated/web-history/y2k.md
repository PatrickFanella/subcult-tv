# Y2K / Digital futurism

A visual study of turn-of-the-millennium digital imagery and interface motifs.

## Source observations

The museum groups period examples in its Y2K exhibition. This aesthetic overlaps several years and does not define every website in those years.

[Source collection or timeline](https://www.webdesignmuseum.org/exhibitions/y2k-aesthetic-in-web-design) · [Gallery to explore](https://www.webdesignmuseum.org/exhibitions/y2k-aesthetic-in-web-design)

## Design direction

These design notes and exercises are SUBCULT interpretations, rather than specifications supplied by the museum.

Choose one recurring device, such as a technical frame or metallic surface. Keep decorative readouts separate from real controls.

| Element | Suggested treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Typography | Expressive headings; readable labels |
| Layout | Framing around a clear content hierarchy |
| Color | Restrained base; sharply defined accent |

## Where to use it

A release cover, promotional page or artist identity study.

## Studio exercise

Explore a release page with one technical framing motif. Compare an undecorated version to check whether credits and controls remain clear.
