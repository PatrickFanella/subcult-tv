# Study outline

Adapt a neighboring study rather than forcing every subject into the same length. Include the following where evidence supports it:

1. **Subject and evidence:** site/style/era, source links, observation and capture dates, inspected viewports and states, supplied versus captured material, and access limits.
2. **Design thesis:** the specific visual and organizational choices that give the subject its identity.
3. **Structure:** content hierarchy, grid, density, navigation and responsive behavior actually observed.
4. **Type and color:** roles, values and pairings, with measurement method or estimate labels. Keep inferred tokens separate from official source declarations.
5. **Components and behavior:** representative patterns and inspected states. Label untested states.
6. **Imagery and motifs:** composition, treatment, ownership and permitted reuse.
7. **Application to SUBCULT:** useful moves, likely tradeoffs and original adaptation ideas. These are authored recommendations.
8. **Source register:** source URLs/files, licenses or unknown rights, capture context, retained-file hashes and verification limits.

Use `DESIGN.md` or the collection's existing document naming convention. Add tokens or diagrams only when they make the study more useful; do not invent measured precision to fill a template. A source register can follow the nearby curated `sources.json` convention without copying another collection's dates or evidence claims.
