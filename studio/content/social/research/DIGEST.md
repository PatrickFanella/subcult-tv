# Social drafting digest

Compact rules for the drafting model and the reviewer model, checked 2026-10-05. Sources and detail: [engagement-psychology.md](engagement-psychology.md), [platform-playbooks.md](platform-playbooks.md), [growth-strategy.md](growth-strategy.md), [visuals.md](visuals.md). [VOICE.md](../../VOICE.md) decides voice; the [Product Claims Register](../Product%20Claims%20Register.md) decides facts. Where this file conflicts with either, they win.

Tags: **[P]** platform-documented, **[R]** research-backed, **[C]** practitioner consensus, **[F]** folklore (listed only to reject).

## Rules for every draft

1. **One post, one point.** Why: readers scan and skip; a second point dilutes the first. [R]
2. **Put the meaning in the first words.** Name the product or the specific thing in the first line; no greeting, no throat-clearing. Why: scanners see about two words per line, and Instagram truncates near 125 characters. [R][C]
3. **Open a small gap and close it in the same post.** Why: a specific, small gap creates curiosity; an unpaid gap reads as clickbait and costs credibility. [R]
4. **Be concrete.** Real names, places, on-screen objects and verified numbers. Why: concrete language is remembered and signals listening; vague copy is ignored. [R]
5. **Give the reader something to pass on:** a use, a fix, a fact, a moment of awe. Why: practical value, interest and awe predict sharing; people share what makes them look informed or helpful. [R]
6. **Show the work and the person.** A capture, a decision, a problem solved. Why: visible effort raises perceived value; process posts sustain audiences. [R][C]
7. **State maturity once, plainly** (pre-alpha, in development, private preview). Why: accuracy; overclaiming a demo destroys trust with exactly the people we need. [VOICE.md]
8. **No invented numbers, users, quotes, urgency or guarantees.** Why: fluent false claims are believed more easily, which makes them more damaging. [R]
9. **No engagement bait.** No "like if", "comment YES", "tag a friend", "RT to", fake polls, or questions the next line answers. Why: Meta demotes it; X weights "not interested" at −47.52 and mute at −58.8 against 0.5 for a like (probability-weighted). [P]
10. **A question only when we want the answers.** Why: replies are weighted well on X (5.0, more from mutual follows), but a bait question invites "not interested". [P]
11. **Call to action only when there is a next step**, and then a specific one ("try the search", "report the bug here"). Why: specific asks work; generic asks are bait or noise. [R][C]
12. **Avoid outrage as a tactic.** Criticize a specific actor with a source; do not write for dunks. Why: out-group animosity drives shares and erodes trust; people say they do not want it amplified. [R]
13. **Write like a person, not a model.** Vary sentence shape; avoid VOICE.md's banned words and repeated shapes; no "not just X, but Y"; no summary closer. Why: readers trust emotional copy less when they suspect AI, especially next to human posts. [R]
14. **Hashtags: zero by default, at most two specific ones.** Why: Instagram caps at five and says tags help search, not reach; X recommends at most two; on Bluesky, use a tag only if a known feed selects on it. [P][C]
15. **Every image gets alt text** that conveys meaning, includes any on-image text, and does not repeat the caption. Why: accessibility; Bluesky users expect it. [P][R]
16. **Real captures over polished or generated images.** Generated art only for concepts, never fake screens, news scenes or real people. Why: Instagram's head calls rawness the new authenticity signal; Meta labels AI images. [P][R]
17. **Separate copy per account.** Patrick: first person, software and music, politics. SUBCULT: "we", products and independent culture. clpr: clips only. Why: X forbids duplicate posts across one operator's accounts; voices must differ. [P][VOICE.md]

## Per platform

- **Instagram:** image or short carousel does the stopping; first caption line survives truncation; write for sends ("who would forward this?"); no caption URLs, name the site in plain text or point to the bio; original material only; 4:5 master with the subject inside a centered 3:4 crop. Sends per reach drive non-follower reach. [P]
- **X:** 280 characters, text-only is fine; earn replies and link-copy shares; space posts out (author-diversity decay, 48-hour window); links inline when needed, since X says the link penalty ended about mid-2025. [P]
- **Bluesky:** 300 graphemes; links welcome and need a real card (title, description, thumbnail); alt text mandatory in practice; no corporate tone; starter packs and topic feeds drive discovery. [P][R][C]
- **Patreon:** frank ledger: what we worked on, what broke, what support paid for, one thing to try or answer. Public editions double as discovery. Describe the rhythm without promising dates. [P][VOICE.md]
- **Devlog:** the long canonical version. Title says what changed for whom; problem, change, what is still unfinished, how to try it; dated; one real capture with alt text near the top. [R][C]

## Reject these myths

- "Sends are worth 3–5 likes." No source. [F]
- "Put links in replies on X." X says the penalty is gone. [F]
- "One report cancels 468 likes." X's code comments call this wrong. [F]
- "Eight-second attention span." Untraceable. [F]
- "85% of video is watched muted." Publisher self-reports from 2016. [F]
- "Post four times a week on Patreon" or any universal best time. No traceable source. [F]
- "Hashtags grow reach." Instagram says no. [P]

## Reviewer rubric

Score each criterion 1–5. A draft passes with no hard fail, no criterion below 3, and a total of at least 30 of 40.

| # | Criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **First line** | Greeting, filler, or the point arrives late | Point is in the first line but generic | First words name the specific thing and open a small gap the post closes |
| 2 | **Specificity** | Abstract claims ("powerful", "own your data") | Some concrete detail | Names, on-screen objects, verified numbers; a reader could picture it |
| 3 | **Reason to care** | No person or problem | Problem implied | A person, the problem they meet, and what is different now |
| 4 | **Reader value** | Pure announcement or self-promotion | Mildly useful | Something the reader can use, learn or would forward to someone specific |
| 5 | **Voice fit** | Wrong account voice or sounds like a model | Right voice, some stock shapes | Unmistakably this account and product per VOICE.md; shape differs from the rest of the batch |
| 6 | **Clarity and fit to platform** | Over length, wall of text, or wrong format | Readable, minor issues | Fits the platform's limits and truncation, scannable, image/alt text right |
| 7 | **Honest maturity and claims** | Overstates readiness or makes an unregistered claim | Accurate but buries the limit | Every claim in the register; maturity stated once, plainly, where it matters |
| 8 | **Call to action fit** | Bait, or a generic ask | Reasonable ask, slightly vague | Either no ask (and none needed) or one specific next step that matches the reader's state |

**Hard fails (reject regardless of score):** invented fact, number, user or quote; claim missing from the claims register; dropped maturity limit; engagement bait; identical text to another account's draft; image without alt text; generated image of a product screen, real person or news scene; review-pipeline vocabulary (merged, PR, deployment, verification) in public copy; political claim without a source.

**Reviewer output:** the eight scores, any hard fail, the single change that would raise the lowest score, and a rewritten first line if criterion 1 scored below 4. Do not rewrite the whole post unless asked.
